## Persona

- **The club photographer or secretary** who drops in a folder of match-day photos and wants them live in a minute.
- **A prospective family** browsing the club before joining.

## Why it was built

The Wix site had a fixed handful of photos edited through the Wix editor. The redesign spec required a gallery that non-technical committee members can update without a developer.

## What it does

- `/gallery` shows a responsive photo grid with a count and a lightbox dialog on click. The newest upload is also the Open Graph share image for the page.
- The first six photos (newest first) also appear on the home page teaser.
- New photos go to the front: if no position is given the upload is placed before the current lowest position. Captions are optional (a few words). Position is an admin-only field.
- Uploads: Payload's native bulk upload on the gallery list ("Bulk Upload") or a single upload. In production files go straight from the browser to Vercel Blob (images up to 15 MB); locally they go to `./gallery-photos`.
- Read access is public; create, update and delete need a signed-in committee member. Saving revalidates `/gallery` and `/`.
- `payload/scripts/add-gallery-photos.ts` bulk-adds a folder of images from the command line (guarded, idempotent, local by default).

## How to use it

1. Admin: **Photo gallery**, then **Bulk Upload** (or "Add photos to the gallery" on Home).
2. Drag the pictures in and save. Add captions if you like.
3. To remove one, open it and delete.

## Not included / deferred

- No albums, tags or per-event galleries (event recap photos live on events, see [event photos](./event-photos.mdx)).
- No server-side resizing of originals; the browser optimises only on the public forms. Tracking issue: [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
