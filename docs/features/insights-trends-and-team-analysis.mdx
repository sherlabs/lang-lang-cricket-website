## Persona

- **A player or parent** who wants to see how a season is going innings by innings, and whether this season looks different from the last.
- **A captain or selector** who wants to know how the team really wins: batting first or second, by how much, with what totals.
- **The club historian** comparing seasons.

## Why it was built

The leaderboards answer "who has the most". These pages answer "how is it going", from the stored games, without inventing anything (issues [#33](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/33) and [#34](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/34); tracking issue [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38)). Nothing names a club: names, colours and grade labels come from the site settings and theme.

## What it does

One **Insights** entry in the Stats menu opens two views, switched by the tabs under the menu.

**Team analysis** (`/stats/insights`, filters for season and grade): record with batting first against batting second and by the toss; winning margins (runs when the winner batted first, wickets in hand when the winner chased, in buckets); a batting profile (average, highest and lowest completed total, all-out rate, wickets lost, extras, fifties and hundreds, inferred opening stands); a bowling profile (average conceded, opposition all out, wides and no balls, how wickets came by type); typical winning totals per grade; fielding (catches, stumpings, run outs).

**Trends** (`/stats/insights/trends`): the last ten results, average team total for and against per season, our total when batting first with a rolling average, and a list of players with at least 3 stored games linking to their page.

**A player's trends** (`/stats/insights/players/[slug]`, linked from the profile): runs in each innings as bars (a not out is an outlined bar with a star, a duck is shown) with a rolling average of the last 5 innings; wickets in each spell with a rolling economy; a season-by-season table; and **Compare seasons**, where a "higher", "lower" or "about the same" label appears only when both seasons have enough innings (or overs) and differ by more than 15 percent of the figure compared with.

**The honesty rules.**

- Every figure shows its sample (games, innings or spells). A figure with too few behind it reads n/a and the count shows instead; a missing figure is never counted as zero.
- An average is runs per dismissal. With no dismissal it reads "not out", never a number. The rolling average is drawn only where its window holds 3 dismissals; otherwise it is a gap.
- Strike rate and economy count only innings or spells with balls recorded.
- Who batted first comes from the stored innings order, not the toss. A game with no clear order, with more than one innings each, with no totals, a forfeit or a draw is not put in a margin bucket and is counted as "margin not comparable" with the reason.
- A spread of margins or a typical winning total shows from 5 games.
- Imported history games are used wherever they have the field (totals) and left out, with a count, where they do not (extras, fall of wickets, toss). This includes winning margins: an imported game with totals that fit its result is bucketed like any other, and one without usable totals is counted as "margin not comparable".
- A stored game with no season name is shown as "No season recorded". It stays in the Season filter and in a player's season table, and is left out of the season chart because it is not a season.
- A player's average here is runs per dismissal over innings with a recorded status. An innings with no recorded status is left out, so the figure can differ slightly from the average on the profile and the leaderboards, which count such an innings as an out.
- The sample sizes for these pages (`minInnings`, `minBalls`) are their own settings in `config/site.ts`; changing the batting-position or opposition minimums in Site settings does not move them.
- Everything is club side only: the pages never show an opposition player. A hidden player appears nowhere and their trends address is a 404. Per-innings dates and opposition are shown only for grades shown by default on the site; a player whose games are all in other categories (for example junior) sees season totals only.
- Charts are inline SVG with a title and a description, and a "Show as table" with the same numbers.

## How to use it

1. Open **Stats**, then **Insights**. Use Season and Grade, then Apply.
2. Open the **Trends** tab, then a player's name for their page; or use "Trends and season comparison" on a player profile.
3. On a player's page pick two seasons under Compare seasons.

## Not included / deferred

- Forecasts of any kind (milestone projections, breakout or decline verdicts): a label compares two numbers only. The existing milestones page shows who is approaching a milestone ([#33](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/33)).
- A post-match review ([#34](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/34)).
- Anything per delivery: phases, wagon wheels, strike rotation ([#35](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/35)).
- Adjusting a result for the strength of the opposition.
- Dropped catches (the scorer does not record them), and any export.
- Junior games are not in the match data.
