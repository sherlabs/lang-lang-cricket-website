## Persona

- **The secretary** who records "Club champion 2024/25" against the winner after presentation night.
- **A life member** or visitor who wants to see every premiership and award in one place.

## Why it was built

Honours were already stored per player (title and years). The honour board (BetterStats feature A7) aggregates them into one club-wide page instead of a separate list that would have to be kept in step.

## What it does

- Aggregates `players.honours` by normalised title (trimmed, spaces collapsed, case-insensitive) and lists the recipients with their years.
- Two views: by honour (default) and by year (`?view=`), with category chips (`?cat=`). Default categories are keyword-based: Life Member, Hall of Fame, Premiership, Best and Fairest / Club Champion, Captain, Leadership / Role, Association honours and Other. An admin can replace them in Site settings, Stats tab ("Honour categories": a label and keywords).
- Years are parsed for sorting (4-digit years and `2024/25` style ranges); anything unparsable sorts last and still displays as typed.
- Hidden players are excluded. Empty state when no honours exist. Filtered views are `noindex`.
- Season yearbooks reuse the same honours to show the ones whose years mention that season.

## How to use it

1. Admin: **Players**, open the player, add an entry under Honours (title, years), save.
2. Use the same wording each time so people group together (for example always "Club champion").

## Not included / deferred

- Honours are free text, so classification is imperfect; fix by editing the keywords or the text.
- Honours are not linked to a specific award event or photo; honours for people who were never players cannot be recorded here. Tracking issue: [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
