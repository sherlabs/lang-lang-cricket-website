## Persona

- **A parent or member** who sees "Presentation night, Saturday 15 Nov" and wants to say "yes, 3 of us" or "can't make it", and pick a dinner, without creating an account.
- **The social secretary** who needs to know how many are coming, who wants beef or veg, and who has paid via the ticket link.
- **A coach** running a weekly "Thursday training, 6pm until March" series that people RSVP to one week at a time.

## Why it was built

The old site had no way to count attendance for dinners and working bees; organisers used Facebook comments and text messages. The events spec (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-24-events-design.md`) asked for one-time and recurring events with a no-login RSVP and a private link to change it later. Dinner choices and a payment link were added after the spec (the spec first ruled them out), and the Payload migration moved everything into the `events` and `event-rsvps` collections.

## What it does

**Public**
- `/events` lists upcoming items and past one-time events. Upcoming means every future one-time event plus the occurrences of each running recurring series in the next 6 weeks (`UPCOMING_WEEKS_AHEAD` in `lib/events-queries.ts`). A recurring series never appears under "past".
- `/events/[id]` shows the event, a public tally (going / not going counts only, never names, emails or notes) and the RSVP panel. `/events/[id]/rsvp` is the form for one occurrence.
- The form takes name (required), email and note (optional), and a Going / Not going answer. If the event has dinner choices, a going guest must say yes/no to dinner and pick one of the listed options (`resolveMeal` in `lib/events-meal.ts`); a "not going" answer never carries a meal. Text limits: name 100, email 200, note 1000 characters.
- Occurrences are computed, not stored (`lib/event-occurrences.ts`). A recurring event is one weekday, one start time, a first week and a last week. The server only accepts a date that is a real, future occurrence of that event.
- After submitting, a private link `/events/rsvp/[token]` lets the person change their answer, name, email, note and meal, or cancel. The token is an unguessable UUID. The same browser also remembers its RSVPs in an httpOnly cookie (`llcc_rsvps`, one year, newest 30 kept), so returning to the form edits the same row instead of making a second one. Lost links are not recoverable by the visitor; a committee member can edit or delete any RSVP in the admin.
- Edit-by-link also works for past events (so people can see what they sent), but new RSVPs are only offered for future occurrences.
- Event pages emit `Event` structured data (see [SEO](./seo.mdx)). `/events/rsvp/*` and `/events/*/rsvp` are disallowed in `robots.txt`.

**Admin**
- Committee fills in a plain form: type (one-time or weekly), date or weekday/first/last week, start time (24-hour), where, description, picture. A closed "Dinner and tickets (optional)" section holds the dinner choices and the payment button wording and link (http/https only).
- The event form shows a read-only "Who is coming" summary and the going count appears in the list. The RSVPs collection itself is hidden from editors (admins can see and edit it).
- Saving or deleting revalidates `/events`, `/events/[id]` and `/`. Deleting an event also deletes its RSVPs and photos. Editing an RSVP through its public link revalidates the event pages; cancelling skips revalidation (the pages are dynamic).

**Data**: reads `events`, `event-rsvps`, `event-photos`; writes `event-rsvps` through the Local API with an allowlist of fields. Token writes find the row by token with `limit: 1` and write by id, never by `where`.

## How to use it

1. Admin sidebar: **Events**, then **Add new** (or the "Add an event" tile on Home).
2. Choose "One time" or "Every week", fill in the date or the weekday and the first and last week, the time and where.
3. Optional: open "Dinner and tickets", type the dinner choices (for example Beef, Chicken, Veg) and a payment link.
4. Save. Open the event again later to see **Who is coming**.
5. To cancel someone who phoned in, open the event's RSVPs (admin) and delete their row.

## Not included / deferred

- No email of the edit link or "resend my link" flow; no email notifications at all.
- No ticket quantities or payment tracking (the payment link just opens an external page). Fee ledgers are tracked in [#25](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/25).
- Recurring events are one weekday per series; no skipped dates or arbitrary patterns.
- A member directory that could pre-fill names is [#24](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/24). Tracking issue: [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
