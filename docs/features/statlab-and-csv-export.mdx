## Persona

- **A coach** who wants "all-rounders with at least 200 runs and 15 wickets, sorted by wickets" and to paste it into a spreadsheet.
- **A selector** building a shortlist of economical bowlers.

## Why it was built

Leaderboards answer fixed questions. StatLab-lite (BetterStats feature A11) lets people choose columns, filters, sorting and minimum thresholds, and download the result, without a database of saved reports.

## What it does

- `/statlab` is a report builder driven entirely by the URL: `scope` (career since 2023/24, one row per player per season, or one row per player-season-team row), season, grade, categories, columns (`cols`, at most 12 from the 26 metrics), sort (`sort=runs.desc`), per-metric minimums (`min.runs=100`), name search and an "active players only" option.
- 12 presets (each just a query string): Top run scorers, Best batting averages, Hard hitters, Strike bowlers, Economical bowlers, Maiden makers, Best seasons, All-rounders, Catching machines, Most capped, Rising stars, Active squad snapshot.
- "Save report" is the URL; there is no stored report. Unknown parameters are dropped and values clamped.
- The page shows up to 500 rows. `/statlab/export?...` with the same parameters downloads a CSV of up to 5,000 rows (a first-line `# truncated to 5000 of N rows` and `X-Export-Truncated: 1` if cut). Non-canonical query strings get a 308 redirect to the canonical form so each report is one cache entry.
- CSV safety: text cells starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab or carriage return get a leading apostrophe; overs and best bowling are written as text so spreadsheets do not turn them into dates. The filename uses only the whitelisted scope.
- Caching and privacy: export is `Cache-Control: public, s-maxage=60` (short, because hiding a player is a privacy action), `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`; robots.txt disallows the export, the sitemap lists `/statlab` only. Hidden players are excluded from both.
- Known quirks: the overs minimum is in true decimal overs (balls divided by 6); minimums are rounded to 0.1.

## How to use it

1. Open **StatLab** from the stats sub-navigation, pick a preset or choose columns and thresholds, press apply.
2. Press the export button for a CSV, or copy the page address to share the report.

## Not included / deferred

- No saved or named reports, filters by opponent, format or decade, or a preset library beyond the 12 ([#8](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/8)). Opponent filters need per-match data ([#2](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/2)).
- No in-app rate limiter; CDN caching and row/column caps bound the cost (a firewall rule is a deployment setting). Tracking issue: [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
