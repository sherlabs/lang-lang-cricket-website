## Persona

- **A volunteer secretary in her sixties** who needs to add an event, post an announcement, change a phone number and approve a story without training.
- **The site admin** who also needs Users, Club details and technical collections but does not want volunteers seeing them.

## Why it was built

Payload's default admin is built for developers (grouped sidebar, API tabs, technical field names). The committee are not technical. The design in `2026-10-03-simple-admin-design.md` makes the admin a plain-English tool: five or six jobs, a friendly Home, no jargon, no way to break anything.

## What it does

- **Roles**: `editor` (committee) and `admin` (site owner). Access rules are unchanged by the simple admin; the menu is only a view. Editors cannot read other users, change roles, or open hidden collections even by typing the URL (hidden entities 404).
- **Menu grouped by task** (`payload/admin/navigation.ts`, look in [Admin design system](./admin-design-system.mdx)): the entries are unchanged and sit under plain headings. **Today**: Home and Waiting for approval (with a count badge). **Club website**: Events, Announcements, News, Photo gallery, Sponsors, Pages and Documents. **People**: Players, Committee & contacts, Player sponsors and Club apparel link. **Running the club**: Club office. **Support**: Help. Every entry has an icon. The headings are labels, not menu entries, so the menu is no longer than before. There is no search box in the menu (about fifteen entries do not need one). **Club office** is one entry for the whole back office (see [Club office](./club-office.mdx)). Editors also see five Advanced entries: **Club history stories** and **Season yearbooks** (occasional content, moved out of the everyday list to keep it short on a phone), **Selectors** (create and manage selector logins, also a Home tile "Add a selector"; see [Selector accounts](./selector-accounts.mdx)) and **Social posts** (pictures and captions for Facebook, Instagram and X, now with a scheduling queue, also a Home tile "Make a social post"; see [Social graphics](./social-graphics.mdx) and [Social posting queue](./social-posting-queue.mdx)) and **Fantasy cricket** (see [Fantasy cricket](./fantasy-cricket.mdx)). Admins also get Users, Club details, Club office settings, Site settings, Site look, Media library, Event photos, RSVPs, Player aliases, Player seasons, Sync runs, Saved reports and Player data tools (CSV import and export of history, and the duplicate-players list) and Social graphics settings (picture style, sponsor logos, footer).
- **Home** (`views.dashboard`), top to bottom: a greeting with one sentence for today ("Two things are waiting for you." or "You're all caught up."); **Needs your attention**, drawn only when there is something to do, one plain sentence per row with a count and one button; **Quick actions**, six big buttons (Make a social post, Add an event, Post news, Add photos to the gallery, Post an announcement, Add a sponsor) and a closed **More** list for the rest; **Season at a glance** (the leading senior team and grade, its record, next game, last result, from stored data, shown with the date of the last update and left out when that data is more than 21 days old); and **Milestones**, up to four players close to a round number. Ladder position is not shown yet: the public ladder is fetched live and nothing stores it, so the card has no fresh same-grade ladder to quote ([#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38)). Administrators also get a closed **Advanced** area holding **System health**: the latest stats update with its counts and error, the sync controls, the old count cards and the Refresh PlayHQ data button. See [Admin notification centre](./admin-notifications.mdx).
- **Help** (`payload/admin/helpContent.ts`): step-by-step guides for the common jobs; edit that file to change the wording.
- **Forms**: plain labels, helper text and placeholders; important fields first; system fields (slug, source, tokens, sync flags, legacy URL, photo order) are hidden from editors but kept in the data. List views have two to four columns and search the main field. The Duplicate action and the API tab are removed. Delete confirmations and validation messages are rewritten in plain English (`payload/admin/copy.ts`). A light, warm theme in the club's own colours with 16px text and 44px buttons on a phone (`payload/components/admin-theme.css`; see [Admin design system](./admin-design-system.mdx)).
- **The classification rule**: `tests/admin-visibility.test.ts` fails unless every collection and global has `admin.group: false`, plain labels and an entry in `everydayNav` (committee) or `advancedNav` (admin only, with `admin.hidden: hiddenFromEditors`). The test also pins the maximum size of the menu (17) and the tile row (14); Pages and News raised them from 14 and 10 because the committee runs both, and Club office raised the menu from 16 to 17 as the single entry for all of the back-office modules. Club office data (`officeCollections`) is never in a menu: admin-only collections are hidden from editors (which also blocks their native pages) and the rest are reached from the Club office page.
- **Loading and signing in** (wave G): a hard load of `/admin` or of any admin page shows a quiet skeleton straight away (`app/(payload)/admin/[[...segments]]/loading.tsx`) instead of a blank page, and the sign-in page opens with the club's crest and name, "Sign in to look after the website" and one line about forgotten passwords (the "Forgot your password?" link under the form shows only when the site can send email; otherwise the line says to ask the person who looks after the site). Facebook sign-in is not on this page; it is for selectors only. On a phone the menu opens from a labelled **Menu** button, Escape closes it and Tab stays inside it while it is open. The bottom of the menu says who is signed in ("Site admin" or "Committee") and has a labelled **Sign out**. The colours for the admin come from a per-instance cache that never holds up the first byte: a stale value is served while it refreshes, saving Site look or Club details clears it on that site instance straight away, and other server instances can lag by up to a minute. If the colours cannot be read in half a second the page uses the starting colours for that one view.
- **Signing in never bounces falsely.** Custom views call `requireStaff` first because Payload does not gate them; it sorts each visit into committee member, signed-in-but-not-committee, signed-out, or "could not check". If Payload answers "nobody is signed in" but a sign-in cookie is present, the gate takes one bounded second look (2 seconds, same database connection pool) before deciding: a valid session carries on, an expired or removed one goes to `/admin/login?redirect=...` carrying the page and the choices in its address (only the keys that page uses, so a "saved" message never comes back), and a lookup that fails shows "We could not check that you are signed in" with Reload and Sign in buttons instead of a redirect. Signed-out visitors to any admin page (including `curl`, link checkers and browsers without JavaScript) get a real HTTP 307 to the login page before the loading shell starts, decided from the sign-in cookie alone: no cookie, a garbage, tampered or expired one. A cookie that is still well formed but whose session was removed on the server is only discovered by the page itself, so that one case redirects from inside the page (the browser follows it; a plain HTTP client sees a 200 whose body carries the redirect). The same rule guards Payload's own pages (Home, lists, edit forms) when their sign-in check fails. A signed-in selector sees "This part of the admin is for the committee" with no redirect loop, on the custom views and also when Payload would have sent them to its own Unauthorized page; the selector-area link appears only for a selector. A disabled account cannot sign in at all, so it never reaches that page. `tests/admin-view-gate.test.ts` fails if a registered view does not call `requireStaff` first. The server logs one line per admin render (page name without the address query, milliseconds for sign-in check, theme and view, and the outcome) so slow loads can be measured; it records nothing personal.
- The Player data tools view also refuses everyone but an admin. Code-written collections (`merge-log`, `ai-draft-counter` and the match tables) are listed in `internalCollections`, hidden from every role.

## How to use it

Sign in at `/admin`. Use Home for the common jobs, the left menu for lists, and **Help** for step-by-step guides. Press **Save** after any change; the website updates straight away.

## Not included / deferred

- No email or push notifications and no "milestones crossed" line ([Admin notification centre](./admin-notifications.mdx)). The member directory, fees, rosters, meetings, diary and bulk email live under Club office ([Club office](./club-office.mdx)).
- The committee sidebar has 15 everyday entries and a cap of 16 (`tests/admin-visibility.test.ts` records each raise and why). The next committee-run feature must group entries (for example one "Website content" entry for Pages and News) or move a rarely used one under Advanced. Pages still shows editors the "Published on" and "Menu order" fields; they are plain helper-texted fields, not hidden.
- Upload fields still offer both "Add new" and "Choose from existing" (a Payload limit). Tracking issue: [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
