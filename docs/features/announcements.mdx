## Persona

- **The president** who needs everyone to see "Training moved to Friday because of the heat" the moment they open the site.
- **A parent** who wants one place to find the latest club news.

## Why it was built

Notices were spread across Facebook posts and group chats. The site gains one committee-controlled channel: a banner on the home page and an archive page.

## What it does

- `announcements` rows have a headline, a message (blank line between paragraphs), an optional **button** (a link to a page on this site starting with `/`, or an `https://` address, with optional button text) and a "Show on the website" switch that defaults to **off**, so nothing appears until the committee turns it on. Switching it off takes it down without deleting.
- The home page banner shows the newest published announcement (by created date), its headline and the first non-empty line of the message (trimmed to about 140 characters). It links to that announcement on `/announcements`, or, when the announcement has a button link, shows that button instead (for example **See the teams**). Anything else as a link (`javascript:`, `data:`, `//host`, plain `http:`) is refused when saving and is never drawn as a link.
- A visitor can dismiss the banner. The dismissed announcement id is stored in a cookie (`llcc_ann_dismissed`); a newer announcement shows again (`shouldShowBanner`).
- `/announcements` lists all published announcements, newest first, with paragraphs split on blank lines and dates in the club timezone.
- **Announcements, news, stories and pages**: an announcement is a short banner for something urgent. For a dated article with a picture use [News](./news.mdx); for lasting information such as About or Join us use [Info pages](./pages.mdx); [Club history stories](./club-history-stories.mdx) are long-lived history. The Help page and the collection descriptions in the admin say the same.
- Publishing the teams from the selector area creates or updates one announcement per weekend (see [Team selection and announcing the teams](./team-selection-and-announce.mdx)); it is an ordinary announcement the committee can switch off, and a hidden internal key stops a second one being made for the same weekend.
- Anonymous API reads only see `published = true`; staff see all. Saving revalidates the home page and the list.
- **Admin list and form.** The list says "Showing on the website" or "Hidden" instead of true and false and has Showing and Hidden filters.

## How to use it

1. Admin: **Announcements**, **Add new** (or "Post an announcement" on Home).
2. Write a headline and message. Put the most important sentence first, because that line is the banner.
3. Switch **Show on the website** on and save. Switch it off to take it down.

## Not included / deferred

- No expiry dates, scheduling ([#23](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/23)), email or push delivery, or rich text (plain text only).
- Only the newest announcement is bannered. Tracking issue: [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
