## Persona

- **The moderator** who checks the site once a week and wants one list of everything members have sent in.

## Why it was built

Two public forms (history stories and event photos) hold content until a committee member approves it. Without one list, pending items sit unseen in separate collections. The simple admin design (`2026-10-03-simple-admin-design.md`) added this inbox so approval is a single, obvious job.

## What it does

- One page lists pending stories (oldest first, up to 50) and pending event photos (up to 100) with a thumbnail, who sent them and when.
- Photos have **Approve** (sets approved, shows on the event page) and **Say no** (deletes the photo after a confirmation). Stories open in their editor with Approve and publish, Say no and Put back to review buttons.
- The menu shows a badge with the combined count (`getPendingCounts` in `payload/admin/approvals.ts`), the Home page shows "needs your attention", and the stories list shows a banner when stories are waiting.
- Nothing is public until approved; staff-only fields (submitter email and name) are visible to staff only.
- If nothing is waiting it says "Nothing is waiting. You are all caught up."

## How to use it

1. Click **Waiting for approval**.
2. Read each item. Approve it or say no. A "no" on a story keeps it, hidden, so you can bring it back; a "no" on a photo deletes it.

## Not included / deferred

- No email to the submitter or to the committee when something arrives; no bulk approve; no spam scoring (honeypot only). Tracking issue: [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
