## Persona

- **An editor on a phone after a game** who wants the result, the player of the match or next weekend's games as a picture, saved to Photos, with a caption ready to paste.
- **An admin** who sets the club's look once (colours, crest, font, style, sponsor logos) and expects every picture to follow it.
- **Another club** reusing the same site: its pictures take its own colours, crest, font, sponsors and wording with no code change.

## Why it was built

The site could only make one player card and the lineup picture. Posting a result, a milestone or an event meant redoing it by hand in a design app. This is the picture half of issue [#21](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/21): every graphic type, in the sizes social networks want, in styles that all come from the club theme. Scheduling posts is described in [Social posting queue](./social-posting-queue.mdx) ([#23](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/23)).

## What it does

**Ten kinds of picture** (a chooser lists real choices, newest first; a type with nothing to show says so):

| Kind | Drawn from |
| --- | --- |
| Results | One stored game (score and margin), the same with the top batters and bowlers, or a whole round on one date. |
| Weekend preview | The upcoming games of one weekend: the opponent is the main text of each row, with the grade and venue on a smaller line under it (on the wide size the grade follows on the same line and the venue is left out). |
| Team announcement | A published team (all three sizes; the public lineup picture routes are unchanged). |
| Best player (the club's award word, `VOTE_LABEL`) | You pick the game, then a player who was in that game. Votes are never used. The stat line is filled from that game and can be edited. |
| Milestone | A milestone reached this season, or one a player is close to. Only real milestones from the milestone board can be drawn. |
| Season leaders | The top 3 or 5 for runs, wickets, catches, sixes or games, for the current season or all seasons. A choice is listed only when it would show someone, so nothing in the list draws "No one has qualified". |
| Event promo | An upcoming event, with its cover picture if it has one. |
| Sponsor thank-you | One to four sponsors with their logos and an optional line of thanks. The logos shrink to fit the page, a sponsor with no logo we can draw (no logo, or a WebP or SVG) is shown by name in large type with its level beneath, never as a grey tile or an initial, and the composer says which sponsors will be shown that way. A single sponsor without a logo appears once, as the headline, with its level beside "Thank you". The footer sponsor strip is left off because the sponsors are the picture. The caption names a sponsor's level only when one sponsor is thanked. |
| The toss | Who won the toss and what they chose ("X won the toss and chose to bat"), against which opponent, on which round or date, with the venue when stored. Drawn from the stored toss details of a finished game; no player names appear, so the junior rule is untouched. A game with no known toss winner is not listed, and opening one by a stale link says "Toss details are not in yet for this game." |
| Season wrap | A published yearbook: cover, record, top run scorer and top wicket taker. The cover is left out on the wide size, and when the yearbook has none the title is drawn as text instead of an empty box. |

**Three sizes**: Square 1080 x 1080, Story 1080 x 1920 and Wide 1200 x 630. **Three styles**: Bold (your main colour, big type), Classic (a light card with a coloured band carrying the crest) and Minimal (plain and airy). The picture is composed from shared building blocks, not drawn by hand per combination, so a long list is always cut at a whole row with "+ N more" and long names are shortened by length.

**The club look, never the code.** Every colour is a theme colour or a mix of theme colours; the crest, the heading font, the club name, the website address and the sponsor logos come from settings and uploads. Text colour is never chosen by hand: it is picked for the surface it sits on so it reaches a 4.5:1 contrast on any saved palette, and text over a photo sits on a scrim that is checked against the worst case (white, black and greys between). A second club saves its own theme and gets its own look.

**Who may appear.** Public on the website is not consent to be pushed to social networks. A junior (a player ticked "Is a junior (under 18)", or whose latest season was in a junior grade) is never named, shown with stats or listed on a picture unless staff ticked "OK to post on social media" on the player after a parent or guardian agreed. This is enforced in every chooser, the player of the match picker, every loader and every list (a junior without the tick is dropped from a lineup, a scorecard, the leaders and milestones). Hidden players never appear. A guest in a lineup has no record to check, so the name is drawn as "Guest player". The public website is not affected.

**The composer** (`/admin/social`, three steps on one page, phone first): pick a kind and what it is about; preview one size at a time (only the selected size is made, and a size already seen is kept for the session) with a style switcher and, for some kinds, one editable line (stat line, thank-you line); then **Share or save to Photos** (the phone's share sheet, with Save Image going to Photos; a plain download on desktop), **Download picture (PNG)** and a caption you can edit and copy. The choice lives in the address (`?type=&source=&style=&size=`), so a refresh or a shared link keeps it. The composer reads the address after the page loads (the server can receive it late on a hard load), checks every value (an unknown kind, style, size, a source id that is not on offer, more than four ids or text over 80 characters falls back to "not chosen"), never rewrites the address until it has read it, and keeps `?tab=` and `?post=`. The page heading shows at once; the composer and the queue load as their own regions with a placeholder, the scheduling and caption panels load only when a picture is chosen, and the buttons stay switched off until the page's script has taken over.

**Captions.** (Saving the wording from the composer stores only wording that differs from the standard text, so later improvements to the standard reach the club.) Season leaders in the Minimal style draw larger rows (up to 35% bigger, the 3 or 5 rows you asked for are unchanged) so the plain page does not leave a blank band; Bold and Classic are unchanged. Each kind has a caption wording with `{placeholders}` (for example `{club}`, `{opponent}`, `{score}`, `{link}`) filled from the data; a line whose value is missing is left out; unknown words are left as typed and flagged. "Copy caption" adds the sign-off line and the hashtags. A milestone that is still ahead uses a plain wording instead of a congratulation. Editors change the hashtags, the sign-off and the wording from the "Captions and hashtags" panel on the same page; the standard wording applies when a box is empty.

**Settings** (Advanced, admin only: Social graphics settings): the default style, the sponsor logo strip (which levels, how many; PNG and JPG logos only, a WebP or SVG logo is skipped quietly and the strip simply does not show if none can be drawn), whether the website address shows, and a small line under the footer.

**Access and limits.** The picture, chooser and caption routes answer only the committee (admin or editor). Signed out, a selector, a disabled account, an unknown kind, a bad id and a picture nobody may see all get the same plain "Not found", never cached or indexed. A person can make 300 pictures in 10 minutes; after that "Slow down for a minute" for a minute. Logos and covers are fetched only from the site's own media host, with a 2 second limit.

**Wave D additions.** These three changes finish the picture half of [#21](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/21): the toss picture, the name-only sponsor thank-you and the Minimal leaders spacing. The toss caption wording is one more box in the "Captions and hashtags" panel.

## How to use it

1. Open **Social posts** (Advanced, or the Home tile "Make a social post").
2. Pick what to post, then the game, player, event or sponsor it is about. For The toss, only finished games with a known toss winner are listed.
3. Check the preview in the size you need; change the style if you like.
4. On a phone, tap **Share or save to Photos**. On a computer, **Download picture**. Tap **Copy caption** and paste it into the post.
5. Admins: change the look in Site look (colours, crest, font) and Social graphics settings (style, sponsor logos).
6. To allow a junior on pictures, open the player and tick "OK to post on social media" once a parent or guardian has agreed.

## Not included / deferred

- Scheduling and publishing: see [Social posting queue](./social-posting-queue.mdx). Live posting to Facebook, Instagram and X is still deferred there ([#23](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/23)).
- AI-written captions or match summaries, video or carousel posts.
- A fixture list that includes junior grades, and player photos on pictures.
- Merging two players keeps the safer social-media settings: the player kept is a junior if either was, and is OK to post only if both were (an undo puts the old ticks back unless they were edited since). If the statistics cannot be read when a picture is made, no player is treated as eligible, so no names or figures are drawn.
- A date of birth is not stored on players, so juniors are identified by the tick, a junior grade in their latest season, or both. Tracking issue: [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
