## Persona

- **A club secretary who is a site admin** and keeps the member list, wants one place to start and wants to know the list of people's phone numbers is not on the public site.
- **A committee member (editor)** who will use the back-office modules meant for the committee as they arrive, and who must never see members' contact details.
- **Another club** reusing the same site: the currency, language and the names of the address lines come from settings, not code.

## Why it was built

Running a club takes more than the website: a member list, match fees, volunteers, meetings, the season's jobs, emails and kit. Issue [#24](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/24) starts that back office with the member directory. The back office is described in the wave D design (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-wave-d-back-office-design.md`); this page covers the shared part that every module sits in: one menu entry, one landing page, who may see what, and the log of exports and removals. The rest of the wave is tracked in [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).

## What it does

- **One menu entry.** The committee menu gains exactly one entry, **Club office**, instead of one per module. Every module is a tile on its page.
- **The landing page** (`/admin/office`) shows one tile per module the signed-in person may use, each with one plain sentence about what it holds and one count. Today there are six modules (seven when email is switched on): **Members** and **Fees** ([Membership and match fees](./membership-fees.mdx)), which are for admins only, and **Volunteers** ([Volunteer roster and hours](./volunteer-roster.mdx)), and **Meetings** ([Committee meetings, seats and office-bearer documents](./committee-meetings.mdx)), and **Diary & plan** ([Season diary and strategic plan](./season-diary-and-plan.mdx)), and **Stock** ([Stock and merch](./stock-and-merch.mdx)), which the committee (editor) uses too. A committee member sees only the Volunteers, Meetings, Diary & plan and Stock tiles. Each count says what it leaves out, for example "41 active members have no email (past members and people on the do-not-email list are not counted)".
- **Who may see what.** Admin: everything. Committee (editor): only the modules marked for the committee; members' contact details are never visible to an editor, in the admin, over the REST API or in any list. A selector has no access at all. The public website reads none of this data, and a test fails if a public page imports it.
- **Personal data is admin only, field by field.** Every field on the members collection states who may read, create and update it, and a test walks the configuration and fails if one does not. GraphQL is switched off. A second test fails if a field named like contact data (email, phone, address, date of birth, emergency contact, notes) appears on any new collection outside the office.
- **Spreadsheet downloads are controlled.** A download is a button press (never a link), allowed only for a signed-in admin from this site, and it is written to the log before any data is sent. The spreadsheet writer is the existing formula-safe one used by the stats pages, so a cell that begins with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@` or similar cannot run as a formula. Date of birth, emergency contact and notes are left out of every download unless the admin ticks an extra box, and the tick is recorded.
- **Exports and sends log** (`office-audit-log`, admins read it, nobody can write it by hand). It records spreadsheet downloads (members, fees and volunteer hours), removals of personal details, bulk changes (adding players as members, adding several members to a season, closing a season) and seat handovers at a yearly general meeting, with who, when, how many rows and which filter keys. It never stores a name, an email address or search text. **Opening one member's page is not logged.**
- **Club office settings** (Advanced, admin only): currency and language/region (they start from `OFFICE_CURRENCY` and `OFFICE_LOCALE`, which default to AUD and the club locale) and the names of the four address lines used as column names in the members spreadsheet. The defaults are neutral words ("Locality", "Region", "Postal code"). The fees module adds one tick, "Charge fill-in players match fees" (off by default), and the volunteer roster adds the list of jobs (code, name, usual hours and people, paid or volunteer; neutral defaults Canteen, Bar and Gate), and the season diary adds "Days of warning for jobs that are coming up" (default 14) and the names of the eight diary groups ([Season diary and strategic plan](./season-diary-and-plan.mdx)). Later modules add their own settings here.
- **Access in numbers.** `tests/int/office-matrix.int.test.ts` is the single table of who can do what with each office collection through the Local API and REST: members, membership types and memberships are admin only in every operation; the log and the fee ledger collections (payments, waivers, charges, seasons) are readable by admins and writable by nobody over the API; the volunteer roster collections are readable by the committee (an editor does not see whether an assignment is paid) and writable by nobody over the API, and the per-person hours ledger is readable by admins only; committee seats are readable by the committee and edited by admins, and meeting data is readable by the committee except restricted rows, which only office-bearers read (documents too), and written by nobody over the API; the season diary and plan are club figures, so the committee reads and edits them and only an admin deletes.

## How to use it

1. Sign in to the admin. Click **Club office** in the menu.
2. Pick **Members** or **Fees** (admins), or **Volunteers** (the committee). See [Member directory](./member-directory.mdx), [Membership and match fees](./membership-fees.mdx) and [Volunteer roster and hours](./volunteer-roster.mdx).
3. Admins: **Club office settings** and **Exports and sends log** are the two links at the bottom of the page. Set the currency and the address line names once.
4. To use the office for a second club, set `OFFICE_CURRENCY` and `OFFICE_LOCALE` (see [Multi-club template](./multi-club-template.mdx)) before the first save of the settings, or change them in Club office settings afterwards.

## Not included / deferred

- Bulk email is built ([Bulk email and unsubscribes](./bulk-email.mdx)); its tile appears only when email is configured. Stock and merch are built ([Stock and merch](./stock-and-merch.mdx)). Tracked in [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
- Reading one member's record is not logged. A club that wants that needs a read hook and a decision about log size.
- No separate treasurer or secretary role: anyone who needs members or fees today must be a site admin. A narrower role is a decision for the club, not built.
- The address line names rename the spreadsheet columns only; the form on the member page keeps its standard wording (the admin cannot read a setting while drawing a form label).
- Card or bank details are never stored, and a card-shaped number typed into a note or phone box is refused.
