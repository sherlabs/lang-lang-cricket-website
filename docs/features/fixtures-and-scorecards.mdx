## Persona

- **A parent** on Saturday morning looking for "where and when does the Under 14s play, and who is the opposition".
- **A player** checking the ladder or reading last week's scorecard.
- **The committee** who used to key fixtures in by hand.

## Why it was built

The original design had admin-entered fixtures because PlayHQ credentials were not available. PlayHQ then granted data partner access (`2026-09-16-playhq-integration-design.md`), so fixtures, results, ladders and scorecards are read live from the PlayHQ public API instead of being maintained by hand. The manual fixtures table was removed.

## What it does

- `/fixtures` has a season picker (`?season=`) and a team switcher (`?team=`). The hub shows "Next up" (upcoming games grouped by date), "Latest results", ladders for the club's graded teams with the club row highlighted, and a grid of the club's teams. Choosing a team shows that team's ladder, games and player batting and bowling tables. Team names have the club prefix stripped for display ("Lang Lang B Grade" becomes "B Grade").
- `/fixtures/[gameId]` is the scorecard: grade, round, date, venue, toss, result sentence, a link to the PlayHQ game centre, and per innings the batting table, did-not-bat list, extras, total, fall of wickets and the bowling table. A game that is not final shows the header with "Scorecard will appear once the match is complete"; a final game with no published scorecard says so. Unknown games and games without a Lang Lang side return 404.
- Junior names are shown as first name plus initial (for example "Jack S.") for child safety (`lib/playhq/names.ts`).
- Data comes from `lib/playhq/` with Next.js data-cache tags and these lifetimes: seasons and team lists 6 hours, fixtures 30 minutes, ladders 1 hour, in-progress scorecards 15 minutes, final scorecards 7 days. Pages revalidate every 30 minutes (hub) and 15 minutes (scorecard). Dates are shown in the club timezone (Australia/Melbourne).
- If PlayHQ is unreachable a section shows a "temporarily unavailable" panel linking to the club's PlayHQ page; the rest of the page still renders.
- "Refresh PlayHQ data" on the admin Home invalidates the `playhq` tag so results show immediately.
- Configuration: `PLAYHQ_ORG_ID`, `PLAYHQ_CLIENT_ID`, `PLAYHQ_TENANT`, optional `PLAYHQ_CLUB_URL`; defaults in `config/site.ts`.

## How to use it

Nothing to maintain. After entering results in PlayHQ, press **Refresh PlayHQ data** on the admin Home to see them straight away; otherwise they appear within 30 minutes.

## Not included / deferred

- There is no separate `/teams` page; the team view is `/fixtures?team=`.
- Scorecards are read live, not stored, so there is no per-match database ([#2](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/2)). Opposition analysis, partnerships and phase analysis build on that ([#3](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/3), [#5](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/5), [#34](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/34), [#35](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/35)).
- No live-score push or notifications. Tracking issue: [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
