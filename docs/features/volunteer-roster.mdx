## Persona

- **The volunteer coordinator** who rings round every week to fill the canteen, bar and gate, and wants to see at a glance who said yes and who is already doing too much.
- **A volunteer** who wants to say "yes", "maybe" or "no" for each game day from their phone, without a login, and see which job they have been given.
- **The committee and the treasurer** who need a trustworthy count of volunteer hours (and, where people are paid, a separate count of paid hours) for a grant application, without publishing anyone's personal hours.

## Why it was built

Rostering was done by text message and a spreadsheet, and the hours given to the club were never added up. This is issue [#26](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/26). It keeps a roster for each game day, lets each volunteer answer from a personal link, and turns a confirmed week into a ledger of hours. It records hours only: there are no pay rates and no wages.

## What it does

- **Game days.** One "week" per game day. **Create weeks from the home games** makes a week for every home game day of the chosen season that the fixtures list holds (once per date, skipping removed games and dates that already have a week); **Add a week** covers anything else. A week is a Draft (only the committee sees it), Open for answers (volunteers can answer) or Confirmed (locked, hours posted). An empty week can be removed.
- **Jobs.** The kinds of job come from Club office settings (Advanced, admin only): a short code, a name, the usual hours, the usual number of people and whether the job is Paid or Volunteer. The neutral defaults are Canteen, Bar and Gate. The code never changes once a job has been rostered (the settings page refuses to remove or recode a job in use), but the name can be changed at any time; a shift keeps the name it was created with, so renaming never rewrites history. A week gets a job from the **Add a job** box, and can change the hours and number of people for that day.
- **Rostering.** Each job on the week page shows the people on it and who is still needed. To fill a place the list shows people who said Yes first, then Maybe, then no answer, then No, with how many shifts each person already has this season (paid and volunteer not told apart), and a note when a person said they prefer that job. Only people with the **volunteer** or **committee** role on their member record can be rostered (the same list the editor sees). Warnings are advice and never block: someone on two jobs the same day, a job with too many people, someone who said No.
- **Paid or volunteer.** Whether an assignment is paid comes from the job's setting when the person is added; nobody posts it from a form. Only an admin can see it or change it for one person. An editor sees who is on what, never whether anyone is paid.
- **Confirming a week.** **Confirm week and post hours** adds one hours entry for every person rostered or confirmed, locks the roster (no adding, removing, recoding or removing jobs) and marks the week Confirmed. Pressing it again adds nothing, and the database itself refuses a second live entry for one assignment. If someone did not turn up, **Did not turn up** voids their entry; **Turned up after all** posts a new one. **Reopen this week** voids that week's roster hours (they stay as history, marked voided with the reason) and unlocks the roster; confirming again posts fresh entries, so there is always exactly one live entry per assignment. Anyone who was marked as not turning up stays that way and is not paid again.
- **Personal links.** Each volunteer has their own link (`/volunteer/...`). It shows their first name, the game days open for answers, Yes / Maybe / No buttons, the jobs they would prefer and a short note, and their own shifts. It shows no other person's name, answer or details. The link is built from a secret and the person, so it cannot be used for anyone else and nothing is stored; **New link** on the Volunteers page makes the old address stop working, and archiving a member, putting them on the do-not-email list, or removing their personal details does the same. The page asks the browser not to cache it, not to send it on as a referrer and not to appear in search results, and every look counts against the visitor's address (60 per ten minutes) so guessing links is slow. A wrong, old or blocked link is a plain "not found". A note that looks like a card number is refused. Whoever holds a forwarded link can answer as that person, so send links privately.
- **Hours report** (`/admin/office/volunteers/hours`) for a season. Admins can also pick a date range; editors get whole seasons only, because comparing two overlapping ranges would reveal one person's hours. Everyone on the committee sees the **name-free totals**: volunteer hours and paid hours kept apart, by job and by month. **Any group of fewer than three different people is never shown as a number:** small groups are merged into an "Other" line, a merged line that still covers fewer than three people says "Fewer than 3 people", a total that could be worked out by subtraction is withheld, when the by-job table has to hide a group the by-month table is not shown (and the other way round), because two tables that add up to the same total would give the hidden group away by subtraction, and a paid or volunteer kind with fewer than three people in the period is not reported at all (so nothing shown includes it). The page says what it leaves out: voided entries, people who did not turn up, weeks not yet confirmed and groups of fewer than three; past members' hours still count. Admins also see **hours by person** (volunteer and paid apart), the latest entries (each can be voided with a reason), a form to **add hours for other work** such as a working bee (date not in the future, 0 to 24 hours), and two downloads.
- **Downloads** (admins only, logged in the exports and sends log with the scope and the season's start year): totals without names (the small-group rule applies, so it is safe to attach to a grant application) or hours by person. Neither includes date of birth, emergency contact or notes, and any cell that a spreadsheet could read as a formula is neutralised.
- **Where it shows up:** a Volunteers tile on the Club office page ("N of M volunteer places are empty for Sat 17 Oct (cancelled and no-show people are not counted)") for the committee, and a line on the admin Home page ("N volunteer places are still empty for ...") when the next game day is within three days and has an empty place.
- **Privacy and access:** the committee (admin and editor) can read the roster; selectors, signed-out visitors and the public cannot. Nobody can write any volunteer collection over the API: the forms and the personal link write after their own checks. The per-person hours ledger is admin only in every way. A member with volunteer records cannot be deleted (archive them or remove their personal details instead), and removing personal details also blanks the notes they typed on their availability. Nothing here is on a public page apart from the personal link.
- **Demo data:** `pnpm seed:demo` adds six game days (two past and confirmed with hours, one in two days with places empty, one the week after, two drafts), answers from about a dozen volunteers, paid hours on the bar of the two past days, and a working bee typed in. All invented.

## How to use it

1. Sign in as an admin or editor. **Club office**, then **Volunteers**. In Club office settings (admins) check the jobs once.
2. Press **Create weeks from the home games**, then click a game day. Add the jobs for that day, then add people. People who said Yes are at the top of the list.
3. Press **Open for answers** and send each volunteer their personal link from **Personal links for volunteers**. They answer from their phone.
4. After the day, press **Confirm week and post hours**. Mark anyone who did not turn up. If you got something wrong, **Reopen this week**, fix it and confirm again.
5. **Hours report** for the totals. Admins can add hours for other work, void a mistake and download the figures.

## Not included / deferred

- Pay rates, wages and payroll: none. Only hours are recorded, and "paid" is just a label that keeps those hours apart.
- Staff recording a Yes / No on someone's behalf, a roster that emails or texts people, and reminders: not built. An admin can email volunteers their own link with the `{volunteerLink}` tag in [Bulk email](./bulk-email.mdx); the roster itself does not send mail.
- Swapping shifts between volunteers, recurring weekly shifts and a calendar view: not built.
- Whether editors should see even the names on the roster and the small-count totals is a decision recorded in [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38); the build follows the proposed default (names for the volunteer and committee roles only, juniors as first name and initial, never paid status).
- Hours for children under the junior rule are not handled differently; check the rules that apply where the club operates before rostering minors.
