## Persona

- **The secretary** who changes the treasurer's phone number or adds a photo of the new president once and expects it to be right everywhere.
- **A parent** looking for the junior coordinator's email, or the child safety officer.
- **A committee member who also plays** (for example the president is also a senior player) and has one picture to keep up to date.

## Why it was built

The user asked that individuals are referenced from one place: "if I add a picture to a committee member, it should be reflected everywhere, or update any of the details." The audit in `2026-10-04-people-sponsors-apparel-design.md` found people already came from the `people` collection but through two query paths and duplicated avatar code, and that a person who also plays had two unrelated photos to maintain.

## What it does

- One collection, `people` (shown as "Committee & contacts"): full name, role, section (Senior Leadership Team, Committee or Junior Coaches), phone, email, photo, order number and an optional link to a player.
- One query module (`lib/people-queries.ts`) and one card component (`components/person-card.tsx` with the shared `Avatar`) feed the home page committee strip, `/contact` (Committee and Senior Leadership Team; junior coaches are listed only on `/people`) and `/people` (every section). Initials are the fallback when there is no photo. A test (`tests/people-single-source.test.ts`) fails if another file reads the `people` collection, builds its own initials helper or draws a person photo with a raw image tag.
- Identity rule (`lib/identity.ts`): when a person is linked to a player, the person's photo wins on the player tile, profile hero, share image and structured data; the player's own photo is only a fallback. The player's **name** is never overridden because it is the PlayHQ stat identity (aliases, merge and sync depend on it).
- The person card shows a "Player profile" link; the player page shows the club role (for example "President") linking to `/people`; the player admin form shows a read-only "Also on the committee".
- Names, roles, phones and emails are never typed into the club global or the page copy. Story author names are free text typed by visitors and are deliberately left alone.
- Saving or deleting a person revalidates `/`, `/contact`, `/people` and the linked player pages. Deleting a player clears the link but keeps the person.
- `payload/scripts/link-people-players.ts` proposes links by exact normalised name (case, accents, punctuation, spacing folded). It is a dry run unless `--apply --confirm`, never guesses on ambiguous names and uses the standard database guard.

## How to use it

1. Admin: **Committee & contacts**. Open a person (or **Add new**).
2. Edit the role, phone, email, or drop in a square-ish photo. Save. It changes on Home, Contact and People.
3. If the person plays, pick them in **Also a player**. Their picture now also shows on the Players pages whenever the player has none of their own, so update it only here.
4. Use "Order on the page" (smaller first) only if the default alphabetical order is wrong.

## Not included / deferred

- Links to players are made by hand or the dry-run script; there is no automatic fuzzy matching. Tracking issue: [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
