## Persona

- **A player chasing a record**, who wants to know the current best and who holds it.
- **The club historian** writing the season wrap-up.

## Why it was built

Leaderboards rank everyone; records show the very best in two views. This is feature A3 of the BetterStats design: records derived honestly from season aggregates with the coverage period stated.

## What it does

- Two lists of records with the top 5 holders each (ties are kept together, with a hard cap so a pile of equal values cannot flood the page).
- "Since 2023/24": most runs, highest score (with * for not out), best batting average, most sixes, most fours, most games, most seasons played, most wickets, best bowling figures, most maidens, best economy, most catches.
- "In a season": most runs, highest score, best batting average, most wickets, best bowling figures, best economy, most catches.
- Averages and economy honour the same qualification minimums as the leaderboards. A 0 not out is never ranked as a high score.
- Each entry shows holder (linked to the profile), value, season and grade. A NEW badge shows when the top record was set in the current season (the newest season in the data).
- Hidden players are excluded. The coverage window is stated on the page. The records are cached under the `player-stats` tag.

## How to use it

Visit `/records`. Nothing to maintain; it updates after each nightly sync.
