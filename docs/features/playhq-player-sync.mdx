## Persona

- **The site admin** who wants player stats to stay current without anyone touching them.
- **The secretary** who finds "Jon Smith" and "Jonathan Smith" listed as two players and needs to fold them into one.
- **A committee member** who has just entered weekend results and wants the site refreshed now.

## Why it was built

Career stats need every final scorecard of every senior season, which is hundreds of PlayHQ calls on a cold cache, so they are synced into the database instead of fetched on page load. PlayHQ identifies players only by name per game, so duplicates happen and need a merge that survives future syncs.

## What it does

- `syncPlayers` (`lib/players/sync.ts`) walks the PlayHQ senior season groups, aggregates each final scorecard per player per season per team and rewrites `player-seasons`. New names become new players (slug from the name, `-2`, `-3` on clashes) with an alias row. Junior teams, competitions and grades are filtered by shared patterns (`lib/players` and `lib/stats/categories.ts`); anything that slips through is classified as junior later in the stats pages.
- The sync never overwrites photos, bios, honours, overrides, hidden flags or manual players. It updates the derived "active" flag (played in the latest two senior seasons).
- Triggers: a daily Vercel Cron `GET /api/cron/players-sync` at 17:00 UTC, protected by `Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET` (401 otherwise, 300 s limit); and "Run sync now" in the players list (`/api/admin/players-sync`, staff only). A second sync is refused while one started less than 10 minutes ago is still running. Each run is logged in `player-sync-runs` (running, ok, error, counts).
- After a sync the site revalidates the players pages and the stats tag, and the leaderboard, records, honours, StatLab and yearbook pages.
- **Merge**: `POST /api/players/:id/merge` with `{ targetId }` (staff only) folds one player into another in a single database transaction. Seasons are combined per team, aliases and honours move, sponsorships and committee links repoint, photo and bio fill gaps on the target, derived activity is OR-ed and the source is deleted. The aliases mean future syncs attach to the survivor. Merging a player into itself is refused.
- **Refresh PlayHQ data** (`/api/admin/playhq-refresh`) invalidates the `playhq` cache tag so fixtures, ladders and scorecards reload.

## How to use it

1. Admin: **Players**, press **Run sync now** to pull the latest. It also runs by itself every night.
2. To merge duplicates, open the player to remove, use the **Merge** panel, choose the player to keep, confirm. This cannot be undone.
3. After entering results on PlayHQ, press **Refresh PlayHQ data** on Home to update fixtures and ladders now.
