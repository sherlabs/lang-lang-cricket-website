## Persona

- **Two team-mates** settling a dressing-room argument about who has more wickets.
- **A selector** weighing two similar players.

## Why it was built

BetterStats feature A5: head-to-head comparison from the stored season totals, with a link people can share.

## What it does

- Two player pickers (native selects in a GET form, so no JavaScript is needed) and a shareable URL `/players/compare?a=<slug>&b=<slug>`.
- Rows for career batting, bowling and fielding with the better value subtly highlighted (ties are not highlighted) and a season-by-season split on a shared season axis. `?common=1` restricts to the seasons both played.
- Unknown or invalid slugs show the pickers with an inline error, never a server error. Hidden players cannot be compared. `compare` is a reserved slug so no player can shadow the route.
- Pages with parameters are `noindex` and the route is disallowed in robots.txt and excluded from the sitemap. A profile links to it ("Compare with another player").

## How to use it

Open a player profile and press "Compare with another player", or open `/players/compare` and choose two players.

## Not included / deferred

- No opposition history, dismissal patterns or recent form in the comparison ([#3](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/3), [#4](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/4), [#33](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/33)).
- Only two players at a time. Tracking issue: [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
