## Persona

- **An editor** who wants the result to go out at 6:30 pm on Saturday without being on a phone at that moment, and who needs to see what went out and what did not.
- **An admin** who connects the club's networks (by the people who manage the website, not in the admin) and watches that the scheduler is running.
- **Another club** reusing the same site: captions, hashtags, posting hours and picture style are its own settings; the networks it can post to depend only on the credentials it holds.

## Why it was built

Making a picture was only half of issue [#23](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/23): the post still had to be made by hand at the right time. This adds a queue where the committee composes a post (picture, caption, hashtags), picks networks and a time, and a scheduled run publishes it. It is built so that a post can never silently be posted twice and a failure is never hidden: every post shows what happened, why, and the by-hand fallback.

## What it does

**Compose and approve.** Step 4 of Social posts ("Schedule it") adds: which networks (only those the site can really send to), the picture size for each, the date and time in club time, the words that will go out for each network with its character count, and two buttons, **Save as draft** and **Schedule**. Scheduling is the approval: only a signed-in committee member (admin or editor) can do it, and the post records who and when from the session, never from the form. Schedule is switched off with a plain reason listed under it when: no network can be scheduled, none is picked, a caption is too long for a network (X 280 characters with each link counted as 23, Instagram 2,200 characters and 30 hashtags, Facebook 63,206), the time is not in the future, the time is outside the club's posting hours without the tick, or the site address is not a public https address (networks could never fetch the picture).

**Which networks.** Each network has an adapter. Facebook, Instagram and X are **stubs** in this build: they declare their settings and limits but are marked not live, so they are never offered and a post cannot be scheduled to them, even when credentials are set. They say "Facebook is not available yet. Download the picture and post it by hand." next to the Share, Download and Copy caption buttons. A **Test network** exists for trying the whole flow: it never reaches a real service and is on only when the site is started with `SOCIAL_MOCK_NETWORK=yes` (in production it is refused unless `SOCIAL_MOCK_ALLOW_PROD_DEMO=yes` is also set, and the site will not start with the first flag alone).

**The words are frozen when you schedule.** The caption, sign-off line and hashtags (the first few each network takes) are put together once, at Schedule, and stored for each network. The scheduler sends exactly that. Changing the sign-off, hashtags or caption wording afterwards does not change a post already scheduled. Editing a scheduled post moves it back to a draft (clearing the approval and its words); scheduling again puts the words together again from the settings as they are then.

**The picture is frozen too.** The post stores a snapshot of the data its picture is drawn from. The picture the network fetches is drawn from the snapshot, so a game corrected after approval cannot change an approved picture.

**Publishing.** `GET /api/cron/social-posts` (with `Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET`; 401 if the secret is missing, empty or wrong, and an unset secret never matches) claims the posts that are due in one atomic step, so two overlapping runs cannot take the same post, and sends each network at most once: before the call the network's row is marked as in progress, and if a run dies in the middle the outcome is unknown. The rules per network:

- **Posted**: stores the network's id for the post.
- **Did not post, will retry**: only for causes that prove nothing was posted and may pass (the network asked us to slow down; no connection before anything was sent). Retries wait 5, then 15 minutes, and a network is tried 3 times in all.
- **Did not post**: a refusal that needs a person (the network no longer accepts the connection, the picture was refused). It is not retried by itself; fix the cause, then **Retry**.
- **Not sure it went out. Check the page.** A timeout, a dropped connection after the request was sent, a server error or an answer that could not be read. It is **never retried automatically**, because retrying could post twice. Look at the page, then choose **Mark as posted** or **I checked, it did not go out** (which lets you retry).

Just before sending, the site checks again that the person who approved is still an admin or editor (otherwise "The person who approved this post is no longer on the committee. Review it and schedule it again."), that every player on the picture is still visible, still named the same and still allowed on social networks (a junior who lost the opt-in is not), and that the event or sponsors are still there; otherwise that network is marked "A player's details changed since this was scheduled. Open it, check the picture and schedule it again." and nothing is sent. It also draws the picture once to make sure it can be drawn.

**The picture link.** Networks fetch the picture from a signed link (`/api/social/image/<post>/<size>?rev=&exp=&sig=`) built from the club's site address. It cannot be guessed, is checked before any lookup, stops working when the post is edited, and expires after at most 24 hours (or 15 minutes after the post finishes). It shows only the approved picture.

**The queue** (the **Posts** tab of Social posts, with a count of posts needing attention on the tab): sub-tabs **Needs attention**, **Scheduled**, **Posted**, **Drafts**, each with its count (the sub-tab is kept in the address as `?tab=posts&status=`). With no posts at all it says "Nothing waiting. Make a post to get started." with a **Make a post** button, and an empty sub-tab says so in one short line. Each post shows its picture, the start of its caption, the networks as pills, a status with an icon and a word (Scheduled, Posting now, Posted, Did not post, Draft), the time in club time, and a sentence per network, for example "X: did not post, will retry at 6:45 pm (attempt 2 of 3)" or "Instagram: did not post: The network could not use the picture.". Actions: **Edit**, **Pause (back to draft)** (only while scheduled and nothing has been tried), **Post now** (asks "Post this to <networks> now?" first, then sends one scheduled post straight away, limited to 10 a person every 10 minutes), **Retry** (puts it back in line for the next scheduler check; use **Post now** to send it straight away), **Delete** (drafts, posted and failed posts), **Mark as posted** per network, and for any network that did not go out: **Download picture** and **Copy caption** (the exact stored words) so a missing connection never blocks the club.

**The scheduler's check-in.** Every authorised run records when it ran and the gap since the last run, and the Posts tab says in plain words "Posts are sent by a scheduled job on the server. The last run was 2 hours ago." (or that it has not run yet), and the scheduling step shows "Last checked 4 minutes ago" or "Not checked yet" and, once it has seen a gap, "Posts go out when the scheduler next checks, about every N minutes". It never claims a frequency from configuration. Until it has seen one gap between runs, the composer says a post may go out up to a day after the time you pick, and no warning is shown (a once-a-day scheduler looks the same as a stopped one). Once a gap is known, if a scheduled post is more than twice that gap late or the last check is that old while posts wait, the page and the Home page warn: "Scheduled posts are not going out. The scheduler last checked N hours ago. Download and post by hand, or ask whoever manages the website."

**Starting hashtags.** `seed:club` seeds one hashtag made from the club's own short name plus #CommunityCricket; change them in Social graphics settings. A new club does not inherit another club's tags.

**Posting hours.** Admins set the club's usual posting hours (default 7:00 am to 9:00 pm, club time) in Social graphics settings. A time outside them needs a tick with the reason written beside it.

**Home page.** "N social posts need your attention" (a post that did not go out), "N posts are scheduled for today" and an overdue warning appear in "Needs your attention" for the committee. Counts only.

**Access.** The posts and their per-network rows are hidden for every role and have no menu entry; over the API the committee can read them (never the lease fields) and nobody can write them. Every action re-checks the caller is an admin or editor; a selector, a signed-out visitor and a disabled account are refused and nothing is written. Credentials live only in the server environment (never in the database, never in the admin) and are scrubbed from anything stored or logged; the reason kept for a failure is at most 300 characters.

## How to use it

1. Make a picture in **Social posts** as usual (see [Social graphics](./social-graphics.mdx)), and edit the caption.
2. In **Schedule it**, tick the network(s) shown, pick the size, the date and the time. Read the words that will go out. Fix anything listed under the buttons.
3. Click **Schedule** (or **Save as draft** to come back later). The post appears under **Scheduled**.
4. If something needs attention it appears under **Needs attention** and on Home. Use **Retry**, **Mark as posted**, or **Download picture** and **Copy caption** and post it by hand.
5. Admins, to try it locally: set `SOCIAL_MOCK_NETWORK=yes` and `CRON_SECRET`, then call the scheduler by hand (`curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET" <site>/api/cron/social-posts`). For the site address check on a local site, `SOCIAL_ALLOW_LOCAL_SITE_URL=yes` lifts it outside production only.
6. How often posts go out is the club's choice (see below): the shipped `vercel.json` has one **daily** run at 18:00 UTC, which a free Vercel plan allows. A club on a paid plan, or using an outside pinger that calls the route with the secret every few minutes, changes its own `vercel.json` or pinger; the page then shows the faster rhythm it has seen.

## Not included / deferred

- **Live posting to Facebook, Instagram and X.** The three adapters are stubs and cannot be scheduled to; the live calls need the club's credentials (a Meta app with Page publishing permission and, for Instagram, a linked professional account; a paid X API tier) and are the follow-up to [#23](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/23). The environment names are in `.env.example`; the Facebook ones are separate from the selector sign-in app.
- **Frequent runs.** Posts go out at the scheduler's rhythm; with only the daily entry that is once a day. The club decides between upgrading, an outside pinger, or accepting daily and using the fallback.
- A second person approving (an "awaiting approval" state), "Suggested posts" for results with no post yet, marking a post as posted with a pasted link, story posts, video and carousels, and reading comments or statistics.
- Player merges do not rewrite scheduled posts: if a player on a scheduled post is merged away, the network is marked "A player's details changed" and the post is reopened and scheduled again. A player's `updatedAt` is not compared (the nightly sync touches it); the name, visibility and social-media permission are.
- Tracking issue: [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
