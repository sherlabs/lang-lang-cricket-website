## Persona

- **A parent** who took twenty photos at presentation night and wants them on the club site.
- **The social secretary** who wants to post an official set of recap photos after an event and to stop inappropriate pictures appearing.

## Why it was built

After the club's events there was nowhere to put photos except Facebook. The events spec asked for committee bulk upload of recap photos for a one-time event once it has happened. Public submission with approval was added so members can contribute without gaining any admin access.

## What it does

**Public**
- A past one-time event page shows only `approved` photos in the order the committee set (`getEventPhotosPublic`).
- Photos can only be submitted for a one-time event that has already started or passed (`submitEventPhoto` in `app/(frontend)/events/[id]/actions.ts`). The browser resizes the picture (`optimiseImage`) and uploads it straight to Vercel Blob under `events/pending/` through `/api/public/events/upload`. That route accepts only JPEG, PNG or WebP, at most 8 MB. The server action then checks the URL belongs to the club's own Blob store and prefix and registers it as a `pending` `event-photos` row with an optional caption and submitter name (200 characters each).
- Pending photos are never shown publicly. The submitter's name is staff-only.
- Without a Blob token (local development) the upload route answers 503 and the form says uploads are unavailable.

**Admin**
- Committee members add approved recap photos in bulk from the event form (`RecapPhotosField`).
- Pending photos appear on the event form (`PendingPhotosField`) and in the Waiting for approval inbox, with Approve (sets `approved`) or Say no (deletes the photo and its file).
- New admin uploads are approved by default; public ones are `pending`. Read access is `staffOr(status = approved)`.
- Deleting an event deletes its photos and, through the storage plugin, their files (never legacy files, see [uploads and media](./uploads-and-media.mdx)).

## How to use it

1. After an event, open **Events**, then the event.
2. In the recap section drag a batch of pictures in, or approve the ones people sent in.
3. Or use **Waiting for approval** from the sidebar, which lists every pending photo with Approve / Say no.

## Not included / deferred

- No email to the submitter; no bulk approve; no per-photo reorder UI beyond the stored position (admin-only field).
- Photos for recurring-event sessions are not supported (one-time events only).
- Rejected uploads are deleted, not archived. Tracking issue: [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
