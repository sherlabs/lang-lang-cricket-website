## Persona

- **The president** who writes an end-of-season message and wants a printable wrap-up for presentation night.
- **A sponsor or parent** who wants to flip through the season.

## Why it was built

BetterStats feature A9. The club wanted a yearbook per season that mixes the committee's own words and photos with statistics that fill themselves in, so the committee writes only what a computer cannot.

## What it does

- Collection `yearbooks`: title, season name (unique, for example "Summer 2025/26"), slug (from the season, never changes), status (draft or published), cover, optional premiership headline, three plain-text messages (president, coach, sponsors; blank line between paragraphs), photos picked from the gallery and featured sponsors.
- `/yearbooks` lists published yearbooks, newest first. `/yearbooks/[slug]` renders one; drafts return 404. Every query states `status: published` itself because the Local API ignores access rules; the sitemap does too.
- Auto-filled, computed at request time and never stored: results overview (games, wins, losses, draws, win rate; abandoned games are not counted as played) and results by grade from PlayHQ (omitted with a note if PlayHQ is unavailable or the season is older than PlayHQ), top batters, bowlers and fielders for the season, most games, a neutral "Top all-rounders" ranking (runs plus 20 times wickets, minimum 100 runs and 8 wickets, formula printed on the page and not presented as an award), and the honours whose years mention the season.
- **Match-data sections** (W2, [#13](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/13)), shown only when the season has stored matches (otherwise the page is exactly as above), each with the coverage caption and "N of M finished games stored" (M from the live fixtures list when PlayHQ answers):
  - *Results by grade with scores*: date, round, opposition, our score and their score as `runs/wickets (overs)` (a two-day game shows both innings, `d` marks a declaration), the result letter and word (forfeits and first-innings results are spelled out). It replaces the fixtures-list results only when every finished, non-abandoned, non-derby game of the season in PlayHQ is also stored (or when PlayHQ is unavailable or does not list the season); otherwise the fixtures list stays and the section says it is the source and how many games are stored. The two sources are never mixed in one list.
  - *Win and loss progression*: one inline SVG per grade, a strip of result letters (W won, L lost, D drawn, T tied, N no result, f for a forfeit; never colour alone) above a stepped line of wins minus losses, with a text description and the same numbers in a table behind "Show the numbers".
  - *Partnership records*: the best pair at each wicket and the top five of the season, inferred from batting order and fall of wickets, with a line saying how many innings could not be paired.
  - *Season highlights* (stat leaders, not honours): highest score with opposition and date, best bowling figures, most fifties and best partnership, each from match data. A second "Top all-rounders, from match data" table (same rule as below) appears only when the stored results are complete. It sits beside the existing all-rounder table, which is now titled "from season totals"; the two are never combined on one line.
- A print stylesheet hides the menu and forces a light page; a "Print / save as PDF" button calls the browser print dialog.
- Structured data is `CreativeWork`; the page is in the sitemap. Saving revalidates `/yearbooks` and the page.
- Draft assets are not private: gallery photos and media are readable by direct URL, so do not upload unreleased pictures until the day you publish.

## How to use it

1. Admin: **Season yearbooks**, **Add new**. Enter the season name exactly as PlayHQ names it (for example Summer 2025/26).
2. Add the cover, messages, photos and sponsors. Leave it as Draft while working.
3. Switch status to Published when ready.

## Not included / deferred

- All-rounder *honours* stay committee-entered. AI-written narrative ([#12](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/12)).
- Seasons before PlayHQ have editorial content only. Tracking issue: [#38](https://github.com/sherlabs/lang-lang-cricket-website/issues/38).
